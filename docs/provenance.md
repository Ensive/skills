# Ownership, privacy and licensing

Owned content is distributed under the maintainer's [MIT license](../LICENSE). Preserve that notice and verify rights and attribution for contributions rather than treating public visibility as redistribution permission.

The owned skill distribution is enumerated in [catalog.json](../catalog.json). Third-party skill names in [.gitignore](../.gitignore) are local installs, not owned content; the file also excludes caches and local review artifacts. No third-party skill text, code or license is bundled. Availability elsewhere is not guaranteed. Record source/version/license separately if a future change deliberately vendors third-party material.

The [public GenUI context](../tests/genui-public-context.md) records the pinned public source used to inform fixture structure. Fixtures here are newly authored synthetic examples, not a vendor copy of that application. Do not include employer/customer source, private endpoints, tickets, credentials, conversations or personal records. Extract general engineering practices and keep private project configuration outside the public distribution.

Label simulated data clearly. Synthetic telemetry scenarios do not imply access to operational systems or validated domain conclusions. Public case studies should cite observable artifacts and distinguish measured outcomes, simulations and proposals.
